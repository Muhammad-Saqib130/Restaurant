Our Story

In the heart of the city, where modern elegance meets ancient grandeur, lies ZEUS - a culinary sanctuary inspired by the king of the Olympian gods.

Founded in 2015 by award-winning chef Alexander Kronos, ZEUS brings together the finest ingredients from across the Mediterranean, prepared with techniques that would make the gods themselves envious.

Our philosophy is simple: every dish should be a masterpiece, every ingredient treated with reverence, and every guest should feel like royalty.

The restaurant's interior, designed by renowned architect Elena Theos, blends contemporary luxury with subtle nods to ancient Greek mythology, creating an atmosphere that is both sophisticated and warmly inviting.
